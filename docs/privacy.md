# Privacy

## Student Data Stored

- Email address, name, roll number, batch and active term.
- Course enrollments and section/cohort preferences.
- Student self-marked attendance.
- Personal lecture notes.
- Friend/group relationships.
- Push subscription endpoint keys.
- Calendar feed token.

## Data Principles

- Collect only what is needed for schedule personalization.
- Keep student self-attendance separate from future official attendance.
- Calendar feed tokens are revocable.
- Personal notes are private to the student.
